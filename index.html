Pen / property mask

Activate the pen, then click around the property to place points.
Finish the shape by clicking the first point, double-clicking, Close Path, or Enter.
Drag a vertex to move it. The mask stays put if you click empty canvas.
Click a mask edge to insert a point.
Delete a vertex (keeps at least 3) Alt + click vertex
Clear Mask removes the property shape and erased line sections (asks first).

Outline & eraser

Enable Outline for the boundary line. Set color, width, and optional dashes.
Activate Eraser and paint over the line where it covers a house, barn, or other structure.
Restore Line brings the full outline back. Undo steps back one edit at a time.

Image, overlay & export

Choose a file or drag an image onto the canvas.
Color Overlay tints everything outside the mask so the parcel reads clearly.
Drop Shadow lifts the masked property off the overlay.
Zoom, pan, then Apply Crop. Crop keeps the mask and erased line sections.
Add Disclaimer, edit the wording, then drag it on the photo.
Save as JPG downloads the finished image without tool handles or the eraser cursor.
Tip: finish the mask before erasing the line. Use a larger brush on thick outlines.
